HOW TO READ THIS

Two sides of service health

APP
Application metrics

Request rate, errors, latency, model-call duration, or token usage—emitted by an instrumented application.

DB
Atlas metrics

Database measurements—emitted by Atlas through the OTel Metrics Sink. They complement app signals; they do not automatically create request traces.
